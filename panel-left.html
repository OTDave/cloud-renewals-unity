Employee Spotlight
Jana Galvan
Manager, DRC – SMB
Broomfield, USA · AMER
Accomplishments

National Veterinary Associates (NVA) is our largest data protection renewal contract, and under Jana’s leadership, it has become one of the strongest growth stories in our portfolio.

When Jana took ownership of the account four years ago, the annual renewal value was approximately $500K. Through consistent engagement, strategic account planning, and exceptional client support, she has grown the account by $1.4M over three years, bringing it to just under $2M in annual renewal value.

How Jana Achieved This Growth

  • Proactive, year-round engagement: Jana maintains regular touchpoints with multiple NVA stakeholders, ensuring alignment on usage, evolving needs, and long-term planning.
  • Data-driven account strategy: She consistently monitors usage trends and builds targeted growth strategies tied to consumption patterns and anticipated expansion across NVA’s national footprint.
  • High-availability support: Jana makes herself available seven days a week to address questions, resolve issues, and reinforce NVA’s confidence and trust in our partnership.

Major Wins This Year

This renewal cycle delivered the most significant growth to date. Through multiple strategic discussions that Jana led directly with NVA, the client agreed to a $508K annual renewal expansion. In addition, NVA expanded their OpenText footprint through:

  • ►New product purchases
  • ►Professional Services (PS) engagements
  • ►Server Appliance acquisitions across multiple business sites

These incremental purchases generated an additional $150K in new sales, on top of the $508K renewal expansion.

This is a fantastic example of how strong leadership, deep customer focus, and a disciplined account strategy can drive meaningful growth. Jana’s work, along with the broader efforts of the Growth Team, is delivering measurable and sustained impact.
